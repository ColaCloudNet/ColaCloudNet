## Quick Start

1. Open the [website](https://www.colacloud.net), register, and sign in.
2. Buy a plan under **Purchase** (trial users may skip for now).
3. Follow the guide for your device, import the subscription, and connect.
4. Issues: open a ticket under **My Tickets** on the website (usually within 24 hours).

## Service Snapshot

- **Protocols**: Trojan / Shadowsocks / Hysteria, etc. (per current node config)
- **Regions**: Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, Singapore, Korea, Southeast Asia, North America, Europe, and more (updated over time)
- **Unlocks**: ChatGPT, Netflix, Disney+, etc. (subject to live testing)
- **Bandwidth**: ingress 1Gbps+; typical ~300Mbps+; no rate guarantee at peak evening hours

## Fair Use

To protect service quality, accounts that use more than 300% of average daily traffic in a short period — `(total quota / 30) * 300%` — may be treated as abuse or a leaked subscription, and may be suspended without prior notice. Contact support if you have questions.

Non-enterprise plans are for personal use; we recommend staying within about 5 devices.
